NAME
koray — founder, product engineer, fintech
SYNOPSIS
koray [--fintech] [--applied-ai] [--small-team] <problem>

I am a product engineer who ended up in fintech and stayed, because money is the one domain where being wrong is unambiguous. Wrong number, wrong balance, angry customer. That constraint made me a better engineer than any framework did.

I spent six years inside payments teams — reconciliation, ledgers, KYC flows, the parts of a product that never get a launch post. Then large language models arrived and made a specific kind of work possible: software that reads messy human financial mess and turns it into something structured enough to act on. That is what Koative builds.

My bias is toward small: small teams, small dependency lists, small pull requests. I would rather ship a boring service that survives a year unattended than a clever one that needs me awake. Most of my writing is an argument with a younger version of myself who thought clever was the point.

Outside of work: long walks along the Bosphorus, filter coffee measured to the gram, and a bookshelf of half-read systems books I keep promising to finish.
