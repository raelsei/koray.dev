I'm Koray Güler, a product engineer in İstanbul and the founder of
[koative](https://koative.com/).

I spent six years building fintech and crypto products: exchange web and
mobile apps and trading tools. In 2026 I started koative to build my own.

## What I'm building

- [OpenMacApps](https://openmacapps.com): a catalogue of 900+ open-source Mac
  apps, each with its license and official download checked.
- [OpenBucket](https://openbucket.koative.com): a native Mac app for S3 and
  S3-compatible storage.
- Heedok and workk.me: in development.

Also open source: [Rast](https://rast.koative.com) (image compression in the
browser), [Cento](https://cento.koative.com) (100 website templates) and
[hyperliquid-go](https://hyperliquid-go.koative.com) (Go SDK for Hyperliquid).
