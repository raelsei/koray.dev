An independent studio, not an agency. We build and ship our own software —
starting with Lumi — and lend the same craft to a few client projects a year:
iOS and Android apps, web platforms, and the product design between them.

The order matters. Own products come first because they are the only work where
nobody else's deadline decides how finished "finished" means. Client work funds
that, and stays deliberately rare.
