## Direction contract

THESIS: A contact sheet, not a blog: one B&W portrait and one plain sentence that runs straight into the product list. Refuses the dev-blog index (post list first) and the SaaS hero with cards.
OWN-WORLD: Void #08080a ground, light #f2f1ee type, greys only. Clash Grotesk display set large and tight, Switzer text. Hairline rules, tabular dates. The two koative dots, blue and coral, are the only colour on the site.
STORY: Who (portrait + "product engineer in İstanbul"), Work (live products from koative.com, shipped under koative), Elsewhere (profiles), Now (dated launch order), Log (dated events), the person off the keyboard, then Hire koative.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: sentence in Clash at display size on the left, ending in a colon that leads into the Work ledger just below; the dark portrait bleeds off the right edge and dissolves into the void. Mobile: portrait above, sentence below, Work right after.
FORM: Index ledger rows separated by hairlines; no card grid.
SIGNATURE: The portrait develops in on load (blur and exposure settle, like a print in the tray); the koative dots sit inline at display scale.
